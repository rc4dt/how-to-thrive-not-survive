Hey, gotta start using this wonderful blog my bf made for me so let’s get into it! So I thought of this idea when I was walking home from the gym. That instead of fixating on finding a specific topic to discuss on this blog that I should just start writing and figure it out along the way. I know this originally started as a blog for women’s health and I still want to get back to that at some point. But hey! Health isn’t cured by a one all solution now is it, it’s dependable on a variety of aspects. Anywho that’s not what I feel like talking about, I want to talk why I want to like writing. Recently I’ve been having a lot of language focus in my classes. At first I wasn’t too interested in the intricacies of language because I am a native English speaker and I’m only proficient in one language. Also I can’t lie at first I thought what is the need to know another language if English is the language that dominates the word. I am not proud to admit but I did feel a sense of superiority as I did not have to worry about ever learning this language. However recently my thoughts have started to shift, the question of “does language shape how you view the world?” was brought up. Woah, I never thought of that huh. I know that sentence may not seem like something that would cause a spiral of interest but it was definitely the initiation to this process. Though I didn’t disagree with this statement, I didn’t realize the significance of it. This thought led me to believe that the dominance English has much more detrimental than originally imagined. It’s impertinence is greater then the burning of the Library of Alexandria, but it seems we are not to keen in extinguishing the flames. The endangering of language is the endangering of knowledge.
